02 · Living together

House rules

Nothing strict: just the small things that make everybody’s stay easier.

Quiet hours

Please keep noise down between 22:00 and 08:00. On the terrace you can stay as late as you like, with low voices.

Smoking

The house is smoke-free. You can smoke on the terrace; there is an ashtray on the table.

Pets

Well-behaved pets are welcome. Just let us know before you arrive so we can get the house ready.

Water & energy

Keep windows closed while the air conditioning is on, and switch it off when you go out.

Recycling

The bins are on the corner, to the right of the green gate.

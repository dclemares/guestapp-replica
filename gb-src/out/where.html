01 · By car

By car

Villa Calipso is in the old part of Albacete, a short walk from Plaza Mayor. The street is narrow and one-way, so drive slowly.

AddressCalle Mayor 4, 02001 Albacete
  1. Follow the signs to Centro and turn into Calle Mayor.
  2. Drive slowly: the street is narrow and one-way.
  3. The villa is the white house with the green gate, on the left, at number 4.
You can unload at the door for a few minutes. The Parking guidebook tells you where to leave the car afterwards.

Train, bus & air

Everything you need to reach us without a car.

Train12 minBy taxi from Albacete-Los Llanos

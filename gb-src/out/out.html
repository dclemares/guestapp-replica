01 · Before you leave

Before you leave

We hope you had a wonderful stay. Leaving is easy: you don’t need to strip the beds or do the dishes.

  1. Check-out is by 11:00.
  2. Close all windows and switch off the lights and the air conditioning.
  3. Check the bathroom, the terrace and the fridge for anything you would like to take with you.
  4. Leave the towels used in the bath, or in the shower if you prefer.
The door locks by itself when you close it.

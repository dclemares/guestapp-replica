01 · Food

Eat like a local

Order a drink in almost any bar around Plaza Mayor and you get a free tapa. Do not leave without trying these.

Atascaburras

A creamy dish of cod, potato and garlic, perfect for the cold months.

Gazpachos manchegos

A hearty stew with flatbread and game. Best shared and in winter.

Miguelitos

Little puff pastries with custard that every bakery here sells.

A free tapa

With every drink, in almost any bar around Plaza Mayor.

Ask us for our favourites: we are happy to recommend.

Don’t miss

Plaza MayorTerraces from midday until late, five minutes from the villa.
